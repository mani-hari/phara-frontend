## Payment bug — confirmed root cause (24 Sep 2026)

Browser checkout and the Razorpay webhook (live since 6 Aug) both complete the same cart. Medusa's
`completeCartWorkflow` creates the order and emits `order.placed` BEFORE authorizing payment, and
`POST /store/payment-collections/:id/payment-sessions` deletes existing sessions without checking
`cart.completed_at`. When the webhook wins, the browser's later session-init wipes the authorized
session, leaving the order "awaiting/not paid". Data: 41 of 222 orders since 7 Jul had their payment
created 6 min–4 days after the order (manual "Mark as paid"); 75 orders since 6 Aug show a pending
session created after the order. Fix: backend route guard + webhook-as-recorder + locked reconciler
job + alert email (see `phara-backend-medusa/docs/PAYMENTS.md` once merged).
