## Corrections to earlier sources (don't reintroduce)

- Soorasamharam is **15 Nov**, not 10 Nov (10 Nov is the first day of the
  Kanda Sashti fast).
- Karthigai Deepam is **24 Nov**, not 25 Nov.
- Vaikunta Ekadashi is **20 Dec 2026**, not 31 Dec.
- Thai Pongal 2027 is **15 Jan**, not 14 Jan.
- Saraswati/Ayudha Pooja in Tamil Nadu is **20 Oct**, not 19 Oct.

## Send-date suggestions (Wed/Sat 9 AM IST)

- Navratri (a) Wed 7 Oct; (b) Sat 17 Oct; (c) last call Mon 19 Oct, which
  is off-cadence and only worth it if the owner agrees, otherwise Sat 17 Oct.
- Diwali (a) Sat 31 Oct or Wed 4 Nov; (b) Wed 4 Nov or Sat 7 Nov.
- Skanda Sashti: Sat 7 Nov (before the fast starts on 10 Nov).
- Karthigai Deepam: Wed 18 Nov or Sat 21 Nov.
- Vaikunta Ekadashi: Wed 16 Dec.
- Thai Pongal: Sat 9 Jan 2027. Thaipusam: Sat 16 Jan 2027.
