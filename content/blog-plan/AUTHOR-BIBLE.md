# Author bible

Canonical personal facts for the three bylines. New posts must not contradict these, and
should not add new biography unless the owner approves it. When in doubt, make the detail
generic ("a friend", "our town") rather than inventing a new one.

## Manikandan (the Sunday column)

- A man, married. Wife appears occasionally ("my wife"). One child: a son, around eleven.
  No daughter.
- Lives in a New Jersey suburb (Edison area). Commutes by train or car and works an office
  job with meetings and colleagues. Housing type is left open (apartment and townhouse both
  appear; the family has rented).
- Grew up in Chennai. His mother still lives there and lights the lamp at six; father is in
  Tamil Nadu too, older now, and chants on video calls. Father has had heart surgery and
  a knee operation. A brother in Chennai, and a sister.
- His grandmother lit the dusk lamp in Thanjavur. His grandfather ("Thatha") had a cloth shop
  in Kumbakonam.
- Extended family: aunts (one in Chennai who checks rahu kalam and palangal), an uncle in
  Madurai, cousins. The family astrologer is in Madurai.
- Friends elsewhere in the US (Ohio, Houston, Seattle) are friends, not him.

## Hariharan (the stories)

- Kept deliberately light. He has a niece (seven when she asked about Diwali lamps).
- No city, spouse, children or job stated. He speaks through stories, "grandmothers" in the
  general sense, and the texts. Do not add biography.

## Archana (the practice)

- No personal biography. She writes in the second person and "try this for a week" mode.
- Do not give her a family, city or job. She may say "I find" or "I would", never "my
  mother", "my son", etc.

## Cross-author rule

- An author only says "I have written about..." for their own posts. For another author's post,
  write "there is a separate piece on...".
