A smoke alarm going off, a pot of milk on an electric coil, and a six-year-old in Ohio shouting "Pongalo Pongal!" at the exact moment it overflows. That was my friend Lakshmi's first Pongal in America, and she tells it as the best one she has had. If you are wondering how to celebrate Pongal in USA kitchens, that is most of the answer: a new pot of milk and rice on whatever stove you have, facing east if you can, the family gathered to watch it boil over, a kolam, a rice-flour pattern, at the door, and a thank-you for what feeds you.

## How to celebrate Pongal in the USA at home

The heart of Thai Pongal, the main day, is simple enough to fit in any kitchen.

- **The pot.** Traditionally a new clay pot, tied around the neck with a fresh turmeric plant. In an American apartment, a new steel or brass pot works well. Some families keep one pot only for Pongal.
- **The rice and milk.** Use new rice if you can find it, the freshest short-grain rice at the Indian store, and whole milk. Add a spoonful of moong dal if you are making sakkarai pongal, the sweet version with jaggery, ghee, cardamom, cashews and raisins.
- **The direction.** Face east, towards the rising sun, if the stove allows. If it does not, face east yourself while you watch.
- **The moment.** Let the milk come to the boil and spill over the rim. Do not turn the heat down. When it spills, everyone says "Pongalo Pongal!" Then add the rice and cook it through.
- **The offering.** Put the first portion on a banana leaf or plate, with sugarcane, a coconut and bananas, in front of a lamp, and offer it to the sun before anyone eats.

Why all this matters, and what the four days of the festival mean, is told in [why Pongal is celebrated](/blog/why-is-pongal-celebrated).

## What if there is no sugarcane, no courtyard, no cattle?

Then you improvise, as every family abroad has done. Indian grocery stores usually stock sugarcane in the week before, along with turmeric plants and fresh banana leaves; call ahead, since they sell out. If there is no sugarcane, there is no sugarcane. Put a little jaggery on the plate instead.

No courtyard? Draw a small kolam with chalk on the porch or the step outside the apartment door; many kids love this part, and [the meaning of kolam and rangoli](/blog/kolam-and-rangoli-meaning) explains why the threshold gets that attention. No cattle? Mattu Pongal, the day for cows and bullocks, can become a day to thank whatever does the heavy work in your life: the car, the bus driver, the dog.

The Tirukkural, in its chapter on farming, says the world may go where it likes, but it follows the plough. I think of that line in a suburban kitchen in January, a long way from any rice field. Pongal is a festival for farmers. Most of us are not farmers. We are still fed by them.

## How do you make it feel like Pongal on a workday?

Pongal often falls midweek. You can still make it feel like the festival.

**Before school.** Wake half an hour early and boil the pot at breakfast. It takes less time than you think. Let the children shout. For more ideas that work with young ones, see [festival rituals for kids](/blog/festival-rituals-for-kids).

**A video call.** Put grandparents on the phone, propped against the sugar jar, as the milk rises. In India it will be evening, and they will likely have already made theirs.

**Lunchboxes.** Send sakkarai pongal to school and to the office. If colleagues ask, one sentence is enough: it is the Tamil harvest festival, and this is the dish. [Makar Sankranti and what it means](/blog/makar-sankranti-meaning) helps if a North Indian friend asks why they call the same day something else.

**The weekend.** Many families do the full version on the nearest Saturday: a bigger pot, friends invited, a larger kolam, sugarcane for the kids to chew in the backyard.

## One small thing to carry into the week

This Thai, let one pot of milk boil over on purpose. Stand back, do not reach for the knob, and let everyone in the kitchen shout. Then, before you eat, say thank you for one thing that fed you this season. Smoke alarm optional.
