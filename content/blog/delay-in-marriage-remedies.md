It usually happens at a cousin's wedding. The music is loud, the food is good, and a well-meaning aunt leans in and asks, "So, when is yours?" Delay in marriage is felt most keenly at other people's celebrations, which is why this column begins there. If you are looking for delay in marriage remedies, the traditional ones are well known: the Swayamvara Parvathi homam, prayers at the Kanchi Kamakshi temple, and weekly Friday or Tuesday observances. But before any of those, it helps to look honestly at the wait itself.

## Why marriages get delayed, and what tradition says

Start with the practical reasons, because they are usually the real ones. Studies that ran longer than planned. A career in another city or country. A family's list of requirements that has quietly grown over the years. A previous relationship that took time to recover from. A search conducted mostly through apps and profiles, where everyone is comparing everyone. None of these is a flaw in the stars. They are simply life.

Then there is how Jyotish, Vedic astrology, reads the question. Astrologers look at the seventh house of the birth chart, which is the house of partnership, and at Venus, Mars and Jupiter. Venus is seen as the planet of love and attraction, Jupiter as the planet of blessing and timing, and Mars as the planet whose placement gives rise to the much-discussed manglik label, explained calmly in [Mangal dosha explained](/blog/mangal-dosha-explained). The shadow planets can also come into it; [Rahu Ketu dosha explained](/blog/rahu-ketu-dosha-explained) shows how. Any serious reading looks at the whole chart, not one line.

## Delay in marriage remedies families turn to

When families ask about remedies for late marriage in astrology, these are the ones that come up most often.

**The Swayamvara Parvathi homam.** A fire ritual offered to Parvati as the bride who chose her own husband. Its story, of the Goddess who did not wait to be chosen, is told in [the story of Swayamvara Parvathi](/blog/swayamvara-parvathi-story).

**Prayers at Kanchi Kamakshi.** The temple of Kamakshi in Kanchipuram is one of the most beloved places in South India for marriage prayers. Kamakshi is the Goddess whose eyes are full of love, and many families have made the trip before a wedding was fixed, and again after.

**Tuesday or Friday observances.** Tuesday for Murugan or Durga, often linked with Mars; Friday for the Goddess, often linked with Venus. A lamp, a short prayer, sometimes a simple fast.

**Andal's Tiruppavai in Margazhi.** In the cool Tamil month of Margazhi, young women traditionally sing the thirty verses of Andal's Tiruppavai at dawn. Andal sang them longing for Krishna as her husband, and they are still sung for a good marriage. There is more on the season in [the meaning of Margazhi](/blog/margazhi-month-meaning).

None of these will fix a date. A remedy is a prayer and an intention, not a contract.

## What can you do while you wait?

This is the question I care most about, because the wait can wear a person down.

First, protect your self-worth from comparison. Your cousin's timeline is your cousin's. The friend with the engagement photos may have waited in ways you never saw.

Second, remember why Andal woke up early. The first verse of the Tiruppavai calls to her friends: it is the month of Margazhi, the moon is full, come and bathe, come and seek the one we love. She was not sitting and waiting to be found. She was getting up in the cold and going toward what she longed for. That is a very different posture from anxiety.

Third, have one honest conversation with your parents about expectations. Not an argument. A conversation. What matters to them? What matters to you? Which of the requirements on the list actually matter to anyone? Many families find that half the list falls away once someone says it out loud. There are gentle ways to steady yourself through this in [waiting for the right match](/blog/waiting-for-the-right-match).

## A Friday lamp for yourself

Here is the one small thing to carry into your week. On Friday evening, light a lamp. But do not light it for the search. Do not pray about profiles, or horoscopes, or next month's meeting. Light it for yourself: for your peace, your health, your work, the person you are becoming while you wait. Sit with it for five minutes. The search will still be there on Saturday. You will be a little steadier when you go back to it.
